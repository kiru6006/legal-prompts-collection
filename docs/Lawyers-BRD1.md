### CATEGORY 1: LEGAL RESEARCH

**Business Value:** Reduces 4–8 hours of manual research to 20–30 minutes. Replaces first-pass Manupatra/SCC searches. **Target Audience:** Advocates, Law Students, Legal Researchers, Senior Counsel **Prompt Count:** 40 **Best AI Model:** Claude (primary), Perplexity (secondary for real-time case search)

**Suggested AI Workflow:**

Query → Claude (statutory framework \+ landmark cases) →

Manupatra/IndianKanoon (verify citations) →

Claude (synthesize into research memo)

**Sample Prompts:**

**CR-001** *Title:* Statutory Framework Research Memo *Use Case:* Quick statutory overview for client advisory *Template:*

You are a senior Indian lawyer specializing in \[PRACTICE AREA\].

Provide a structured research memo on \[LEGAL ISSUE\] covering:

1\. Applicable statutes and key sections

2\. Landmark Supreme Court judgments (cite properly)

3\. Recent High Court positions (last 5 years)

4\. Current legal position summary

5\. Practical implications

Jurisdiction: India

Format: Formal legal memo

**CR-002** *Title:* Comparative Statutory Analysis *Use Case:* Comparing old law vs new law (e.g., IPC vs BNS) *Template:*

Compare \[OLD STATUTE/SECTION\] with \[NEW STATUTE/SECTION\] under Indian law.

Cover:

1\. Key textual changes

2\. Enhanced/reduced penalties

3\. New offences introduced

4\. Procedural changes

5\. Practical impact on pending cases

6\. Transitional provisions

Output as a comparison table followed by analysis.

**CR-003** *Title:* Case Law Digest on Specific Point of Law *Template:*

Compile a case law digest on \[SPECIFIC LEGAL PROPOSITION\] in India.

Include:

\- Supreme Court leading cases (with year, bench, citation format)

\- Divergent High Court views

\- Current settled position

\- Minority/dissenting positions if relevant

Format: Digest with headnotes.

**CR-004** *Title:* Legislative Intent Research *Template:*

Research the legislative intent behind \[SECTION/ACT\] in India.

Include:

1\. Statement of Objects and Reasons

2\. Parliamentary debate highlights

3\. Law Commission recommendations (if any)

4\. Pre-legislative consultation notes

5\. How courts have interpreted legislative intent

**CR-005** *Title:* Multi-Jurisdiction Enforcement Gap Analysis *Template:*

Analyze enforcement gaps in \[LAW/REGULATION\] in India.

Cover:

1\. Statutory mandate vs ground reality

2\. Key court observations on enforcement failure

3\. Regulatory body track record

4\. Comparison with equivalent law in \[UK/US/EU\]

5\. Reform recommendations

நீங்கள் \[பயிற்சி பகுதி\] நிபுணரான மூத்த இந்திய வழக்கறிஞர்.

\[कानूनी मुद्दे\] पर एक संरचित शोध ज्ञापन प्रदान करें:

### CATEGORY 2: CASE ANALYSIS

**Business Value:** Converts 3-hour judgment reading into 20-minute structured brief. **Target Audience:** Advocates, Senior Counsel, Law Students **Prompt Count:** 35 **Best AI Model:** Claude (deep reasoning), GPT-4o (for large document uploads)

**Suggested AI Workflow:**

Upload judgment PDF → Claude (structured analysis) →

Extract ratio decidendi → Build case brief → Add to argument bank

**Sample Prompts:**

**CA-001** *Title:* Supreme Court Judgment Analysis *Template:*

Analyze the following Supreme Court judgment \[PASTE/UPLOAD\]:

1\. Case citation and bench composition

2\. Facts in brief (5–7 lines)

3\. Issues framed by court

4\. Arguments — Petitioner / Respondent

5\. Ratio decidendi (binding rule)

6\. Obiter dicta (persuasive observations)

7\. Judgment outcome

8\. Precedential value and impact

9\. Distinguishing factors for future use

**CA-002** *Title:* Adverse Judgment Damage Control Analysis *Template:*

My client received an adverse order in \[COURT\] on \[DATE\].

Case type: \[CIVIL/CRIMINAL/WRIT\]

Issue decided against us: \[DESCRIBE\]

Analyze:

1\. Grounds for appeal / revision

2\. Appellate forum and limitation period

3\. Stay application strategy

4\. Distinguishable precedents in our favour

5\. Risk assessment (1–10) for appeal success

**CA-003** *Title:* Precedent Mapping for Argument *Template:*

I am arguing \[LEGAL POSITION\] before \[COURT\].

Map all supporting precedents:

1\. Direct Supreme Court authority

2\. Supporting High Court decisions

3\. Distinguishable adverse precedents (and how to distinguish)

4\. Foreign court persuasive precedents

5\. Suggested argument sequence

**CA-004** *Title:* IRAC Case Analysis for Law Students *Template:*

Apply IRAC analysis to the following case/fact pattern:

\[FACTS\]

Structure:

I — Issue: Precise legal question(s)

R — Rule: Applicable statute \+ case law

A — Application: Facts mapped to rule

C — Conclusion: Legal outcome

Bonus: Counter-argument and rebuttal

**CA-005** *Title:* PIL Viability Analysis *Template:*

Assess viability of filing a PIL before \[High Court / Supreme Court\] on:

Issue: \[DESCRIBE\]

Analyze:

1\. Locus standi under Article 32/226

2\. Public interest element

3\. Existing litigation on same issue

4\. Courts' current attitude (expansive/restrictive)

5\. Preliminary objections likely from respondent

6\. Recommended filing strategy
