# The Indian Legal AI Prompt Playbook™: Commercial Product Blueprint

From the Desk of the Managing Partner & Chief Innovation Officer  
*The legal landscape in India has undergone a seismic shift. With the transition from the IPC/CrPC/IEA to BNS/BNSS/BSA, the explosion of DPDPA, and the complexity of cross-border tech arbitration, the modern Indian lawyer is drowning in textual analysis. AI is the lifeboat, but only if you know how to steer it. This is not a list of toy prompts; this is a commercial-grade operational manual for the future of Indian legal practice.*

### 3\. AI Strategy & Technology Recommendations

* Which prompts for Claude: Deep reasoning, long-context document review (Due Diligence, Case Analysis, drafting complex SHA/SPA where 100k context is needed), nuanced constitutional interpretation. Claude's "Constitutional AI" aligns well with legal reasoning.  
* Which prompts for ChatGPT (GPT-4o): Web-browsing for real-time case law tracking (Indian Kanoon integration), structured data extraction, formatting heavy outputs (Excel/CSV generation for compliance trackers), and rapid template generation.  
* Which prompts become AI Agents: Repetitive, trigger-based workflows. (e.g., DPDPA Subject Access Request bot, Daily NCLT order tracker, Automated Invoice Auditor, Contract First-Pass Reviewer).  
* Which prompts become SaaS Features: Workflow-specific micro-tools. (e.g., "BNS/IPC Converter Widget," "BSA E-Evidence Certificate Generator," "Indian Contract Act Risk Scorer").
