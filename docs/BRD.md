# Business Requirements Document (BRD)

## 1. Executive Summary
The legal profession faces rapid emergence of generative AI skills. Advocates, corporate counsel, and law students lack a trusted, non-commercial educational workbench to test, understand, and localize AI capabilities for Indian and international law.

## 2. Business Objectives
- Provide transparent visibility into Anthropic's official legal plugin ecosystem.
- Bridge international prompt templates with Indian statutory frameworks (e.g., BNSS, BNS, BSA, DPDPA).
- Reinforce professional diligence: human-in-the-loop review, anti-hallucination checklists, and clear synthetic blueprints.

## 3. Key Stakeholders
- Independent Advocates & Litigation Counsel
- In-House Corporate Counsel & Contract Managers
- Law Students & Clinical Faculty
- Legal Operations & AI Compliance Officers
