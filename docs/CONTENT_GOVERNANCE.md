# Content Governance Policy

## Verification Lifecycle
Every entry in the workbench maintains two explicit, independent statuses:

1. **Educational Status**:
   - `catalogue-only`: Upstream metadata ingested; walkthrough pending.
   - `draft`: Walkthrough written; undergoing internal schema validation.
   - `ready`: Walkthrough validated and complete with fictional scenario.
   - `needs-update`: Upstream skill modified or renamed.

2. **Legal Review Status**:
   - `not-reviewed`: Educational walkthrough created without formal lawyer sign-off.
   - `review-pending`: Submitted for jurisdictional legal review.
   - `reviewed`: Verified by a qualified legal professional for the specific jurisdiction.
   - `needs-revalidation`: Governing statute amended or superseded.

Reviewer stamps are never fabricated. Educational content may be marked `ready` while explicitly showing `review-pending` or `not-reviewed`.
